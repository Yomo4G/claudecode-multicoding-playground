# Handwritten Ephemeral SNS — Contexts

## Background / Why this exists

Modern SNS treats language as information:
things to be optimized, stored, searched, reacted to, and accumulated.
As a result, language becomes consumable, disposable, and noisy.

Many users are exhausted by this environment.
They continue to scroll out of habit, while avoiding posting,
feeling that words have become too fast, too permanent, or too easily misused.

This project starts from a different assumption:

- Words are not only information.
- Handwritten text is not just content, but a trace of a person.
- The world, people, and language are not permanent.

This service exists to provide a place where words may appear,
touch the world briefly, and then disappear —
leaving only a faint trace in human memory.

## Core Philosophy

- Handwritten text carries personality through stroke, pressure, rhythm,
  paper choice, pen choice, and capture conditions.
- Personality should not be reduced to accounts, profiles, or metrics.
  It emerges from continuity, silence, and disappearance.
- Not all language must be understood, agreed with, or optimized.
  Ambiguity and unreadability are part of human intelligence.
- Language does not need to remain forever to be meaningful.
  Some words are valuable precisely because they vanish.

This service is not designed to maximize engagement, speed, reach, or growth.
Constraints are not limitations but cultural structure.

## What this service is

- A public but quiet place, often visited at night
- A space for occasional output, not constant posting
- A place where writing is optional, and reading alone is acceptable
- A system that allows words to exist temporarily, then disappear
- A structure closer to open, ephemeral correspondence than to social media

## What this service is not

- Not a platform for virality or optimization
- Not a personal archive or log
- Not a real-time communication tool
- Not a place for identity construction via profiles or metrics
- Not a tool for maximizing activity or retention frequency

## Users (Conceptual)

The primary users are people who:

- Still look at SNS, but are tired of it
- Rarely post, or hesitate to post
- Prefer slowness to speed
- Feel warmth and presence in handwritten text
- Have experienced the weight or irreversibility of words
- Want to externalize thoughts without demanding reaction or validation

Usage frequency is intentionally low.
Returning occasionally over years is more important than daily activity.

## Success Definition

Success is not measured by growth, MAU, DAU, or virality.

Success means:

- Users feel a small sense of relief after writing
- Users feel that handwritten text has a different weight than typed language
- Users can leave words without expecting response
- Users can read without being forced to react
- Users remember this place and return to it over long periods of time

The core metric is not frequency, but lifespan of the relationship
between a user and this place.

## Time and Rhythm

- The service is aligned with night-time and quiet moments
- It should resist use during transit or distracted scrolling
- Silence, inactivity, and absence are treated as meaningful states
- Notifications and urgency are intentionally minimized or absent

## Worldview

- The world is incomplete and cannot be fully unified
- Meaning is not fixed at creation time
- Understanding is partial and imaginative
- Discomfort and unreadability are allowed to exist

Moderation focuses only on unavoidable legal or physical constraints.
Differences in thought, clarity, or legibility are not problems to solve.

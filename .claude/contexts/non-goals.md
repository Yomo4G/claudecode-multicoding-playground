# Non-Goals — Handwritten Ephemeral SNS

This document defines what this service explicitly does NOT aim to do.
These are not postponed goals or future extensions.
They are intentional exclusions.

## Not Optimizing for Growth

- Not designed to maximize MAU, DAU, or session length
- Not intended to scale rapidly
- Not intended to become a mainstream platform
- Not designed for network effects or virality
- Not concerned with market dominance or user acquisition efficiency

## Not Optimizing for Engagement

- No infinite scrolling
- No algorithmic feed optimization
- No urgency-driven notifications
- No streaks, gamification, or usage pressure
- No requirement to post, react, or respond

## Not Optimizing for Visibility

- No promotion or boosting of popular posts
- No ranking by reactions or engagement
- No trending topics
- No search-based discovery
- No emphasis on follower counts

## Not a Communication Tool

- Not real-time messaging
- Not chat-oriented
- Not designed for rapid back-and-forth conversation
- Not intended to replace email, messaging apps, or social networks

## Not an Archive

- Not a personal diary
- Not a memory store
- Not a permanent record
- Not a place to build a searchable personal history
- Content disappearance is intentional and irreversible

## Not a Performance Space

- Not a stage for self-branding
- Not a platform for self-promotion
- Not a place to perform identity
- Not a space for reputation building

## Not a Validation System

- Not designed to provide affirmation or approval
- Not intended to maximize likes or reactions
- Lack of response is not treated as failure
- Feedback is optional and structurally limited

## Not a Moderated Ideology Space

- Not designed to enforce consensus
- Not intended to filter for comfort or correctness
- Not a place to educate, correct, or optimize opinions
- Differences, discomfort, and unreadability are allowed

## Not a Technical Showcase

- Not aiming for perfect handwriting detection
- Not aiming for absolute prevention of screenshots or saving
- Not aiming for technical completeness or purity
- Imperfect enforcement is acceptable if intention is clear

## Not a Productivity Tool

- Not designed for task management
- Not designed for goal tracking
- Not designed for self-improvement metrics
- Not designed for optimization of thought output

---

If a feature or change makes the service
more efficient, addictive, viral, or optimized,
it is likely violating these non-goals.

The absence of these functions is not a limitation.
It is the core of the design.

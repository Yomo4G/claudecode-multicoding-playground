# Risks — Handwritten Ephemeral SNS

This document describes the ways in which this service may fail,
break, or be misunderstood.
These risks are not necessarily problems to fix.
Many of them are inherent to the philosophy of the service.

## Risk: The Service Never Becomes Popular

This service may fail to attract a large audience.
It may feel empty, quiet, or inactive for long periods of time.

This is expected.

The service is not designed to appeal to most users,
nor to compete with mainstream social platforms.
Low activity is not a signal of failure by itself.

Attempting to solve this through growth tactics,
feature expansion, or engagement optimization
would directly contradict the core philosophy.

## Risk: The Service Is Perceived as “Boring” or “Useless”

Some users may describe the service as:

- boring
- slow
- pointless
- inconvenient
- unclear in purpose

This perception is not incorrect.
The service does not aim to entertain, optimize, or reward efficiency.

Trying to “fix” boredom by adding stimulation,
clarity by adding explanations,
or convenience by removing friction
risks destroying the intended experience.

## Risk: Users Misunderstand the Intent

The service may be misunderstood as:

- a minimalist SNS
- a productivity or journaling tool
- an art-sharing platform
- a mental health product
- a handwriting recognition experiment

None of these are accurate representations.

Misunderstanding is likely and unavoidable.
Over-explaining or guiding users toward a “correct” usage
would impose meaning where ambiguity is intentional.

## Risk: Unreadable, Uncomfortable, or Unpleasant Content Appears

Because legibility, comfort, and consensus are not enforced,
the service may contain:

- unreadable handwriting
- emotionally uncomfortable expressions
- opinions or moods that are difficult to accept
- content that feels pointless or disturbing

This is a consequence of allowing incomplete understanding.
The service assumes that readers are capable of disengaging,
skipping, or imagining meaning without intervention.

Excessive filtering or correction would undermine this assumption.

## Risk: Very Low Posting Frequency

Users may write:

- rarely
- irregularly
- only during specific life events
- sometimes not at all for long periods

This is not considered churn or disengagement.
The service is aligned with human life rhythms,
not usage metrics.

Attempts to increase posting frequency
would introduce pressure and obligation,
which are incompatible with the service’s role.

## Risk: Over-Attachment by a Small Group

A small number of users may become deeply attached
to the service and resistant to change.

While long-term attachment is a success indicator,
it also risks stagnation or unspoken ownership.

The service must avoid privileging “core users”
or optimizing for any specific subgroup’s preferences.

## Risk: Cultural Drift Over Time

As users change, or as new users arrive,
the shared understanding of the service may drift.

Some drift is inevitable.
Attempting to freeze culture through rules or enforcement
would be artificial and fragile.

Structural constraints, not moderation,
are the primary tool for maintaining direction.

## Risk: Technical Imperfection Is Seen as Failure

Handwriting detection may be inaccurate.
Screenshots may be possible.
Rules may be bypassed.

These are not critical failures.

The service prioritizes intention and structure
over technical enforcement.
Perfect control is neither achievable nor desirable.

## Risk: The Service “Succeeds” in the Wrong Way

If the service becomes:

- highly viral
- algorithmically optimized
- heavily discussed
- used primarily for performance or branding

then it has failed in a more serious way.

Popularity that changes user behavior,
language style, or posting intent
is more dangerous than low usage.

In such a case, reduction or limitation,
not expansion, should be considered.

---

These risks are not warnings to be eliminated.
They define the fragile boundaries within which
this service is allowed to exist.

Any solution that removes these risks
by optimization, enforcement, or growth
is likely invalid.
